☀️ Offline Sun & Star Direction Helper

Use these off-grid techniques to find True North without any magnetic compass or GPS:

1. Shadow-Stick Method (Daytime)
  1. Plant a 1-meter stick vertically in level ground.
  2. Mark the tip of the first shadow with a pebble (Point A = West).
  3. Wait 15-20 minutes until the shadow moves 5-10 cm.
  4. Mark the new shadow tip (Point B = East).
  5. Draw a line from A to B: A line pointing from A to B goes West to East. Perpendicular to it points North!
2. Polaris Star Method (Night - North)

Locate the Big Dipper constellation. Draw an imaginary line straight through the outer two stars of its cup and extend it 5 times its distance. That leads directly to Polaris (North Star)!
